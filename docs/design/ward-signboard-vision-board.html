Rules What keeps it from looking generated

Square corners

No pills, no rounded cards. Buttons, filters and panels have hard edges and 2 px borders, like painted metal signs.

Colour is a signal

Blue means you can act. Yellow means late. Red means high severity. Nothing is coloured for decoration.

Plain sentences

Sentence case, no slogans, no small capital labels above headings. Headlines state facts from the data.

One loud thing

The signboard is the only bold moment on each page. No fade-in on every section, no count-up numbers.

Keep  Kannada with English on the signboard and header. The neighbourhood map, sized by complaints. The real complaints, quoted as posted. Dark mode, which follows the device setting.

Drop  The cream background and serif headings. Monospace labels. Rounded pills. Soft-shadow cards. Uppercase labels above headings. The rotating ticker and count-up numbers.
