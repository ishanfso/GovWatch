## 1. Centre for e-Governance, Karnataka (runs iPGRS / Janaspandana)

**To:** [iPGRS / Centre for e-Governance contact address from ceg.karnataka.gov.in]
**Subject:** Request for guidance: helping Bengaluru residents file grievances on iPGRS

Dear Sir/Madam,

I run Civic Issue India (https://civicissue.in), an independent, non-commercial civic-technology project for Bengaluru. It collects public complaints that residents post on X (Twitter) about roads, garbage, power, water, flooding and traffic, filters out the noise, and shows each one alongside the officials responsible for that ward. So far it has collected over 1,000 verified complaints across the city's five corporations.

Many residents who complain publicly never file an official grievance. Our new "Report a problem" page now directs them to the right official channel, including iPGRS. We would like to go a step further and help residents complete their iPGRS grievance from our site, with the resident fully in control:

- The resident signs in with their own mobile number and types the OTP iPGRS sends them.
- They review the completed grievance form and approve it before it is submitted.
- They receive the iPGRS Grievance ID and track it on your portal as usual.
- We never store OTPs or login sessions, delete personal details once the grievance is filed, and limit each number to one grievance per hour.

Before building this, we want to make sure it is acceptable to you and fits iPGRS's terms of use. Could you advise:

1. Whether assisted filing of this kind, with the citizen's own OTP and approval, is permitted.
2. Whether iPGRS offers, or plans to offer, an official interface (API) for approved partners. We would much prefer to use one.
3. Whether there is a published list of departments and grievance categories, so that we route grievances correctly and don't create extra work for your officers.

In return, we would be glad to share anonymised insights from public social-media complaints, by ward and department, if that would be useful to your team.

I would be grateful for a short call at your convenience.

Thank you for your time.

Yours faithfully,

## 2. Greater Bengaluru Authority (Sahaaya)

**To:** [GBA IT / grievance cell contact from gba.karnataka.gov.in]
**Cc (optional):** [Office of the Chief Commissioner, GBA]
**Subject:** Public social-media complaints for Bengaluru, and a possible link with Sahaaya

Dear Sir/Madam,

I run Civic Issue India (https://civicissue.in), an independent, non-commercial civic-technology project. It gathers complaints Bengaluru residents post publicly on X (Twitter), keeps only genuine civic complaints, and maps each one to its ward, city corporation and the officials responsible. So far it has collected over 1,000 verified complaints on roads, garbage, drains and flooding, power, water and traffic.

We read with interest that the GBA is developing a single-window system to track civic complaints across agencies and social media, and upgrading Sahaaya to version 3.0. Our work may be useful to both.

1. **Social-media complaints, already sorted.** We can share our ward-tagged, de-duplicated feed of public complaints, free of charge, in whatever format suits your team, for example a daily file or a simple data link.
2. **Helping residents file on Sahaaya.** Our "Report a problem" page currently directs residents to the Sahaaya app. We would like to help them file directly, with the resident signing in with their own mobile number and OTP, reviewing the complaint before it is sent, and receiving the official Sahaaya complaint number. We would only do this with your agreement and in line with Sahaaya's terms of use. If an official partner interface is planned for the single-window platform, we would much prefer to use it.

We follow strict rules on personal data: no storage of OTPs or sessions, personal details deleted after filing, and limits to prevent repeated or bulk complaints.

Could you let me know whether either of these would be of interest, and who the right person would be to speak to? I would be happy to demonstrate the site.

Thank you for your time.

Yours faithfully,
